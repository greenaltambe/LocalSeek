# LocalSeek Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-01_

LocalSeek is a search app that works entirely on your device.

## What LocalSeek accesses
With your permission, LocalSeek reads on-device data to build a search index. Every permission is optional and is
requested with an explanation; search covers only what you allow.
- **Files** on shared storage (names, paths and extracted text of documents),
- **Contacts** (names and phone numbers/emails as stored in your address book),
- **Installed apps** (names and package IDs),
- **Images** (file names and on-device image embeddings).

## Where it goes
Nowhere. The index is stored in the app's private storage on your device.
- LocalSeek does **not** request the `INTERNET` permission and makes no network connections.
- No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs are included.
- The optional image search uses a model pack that Google Play downloads and installs on demand.
  LocalSeek itself never downloads anything.
- Links inside the app (web search shortcuts, the project page) open in your browser; LocalSeek does not
  perform those requests.
- Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the app, so the index
  is not copied to cloud backups.
- We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

## Your control
- Deny or revoke any permission in Android settings; search then covers only the data you allow.
- Clear the index by clearing app storage or uninstalling. All data is deleted with the app.
- Settings export (optional) writes engines, prefixes, pins and theme to a file you choose. It never includes
  your index, files or contact details, and contact pins are left out.

## Children
LocalSeek does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
