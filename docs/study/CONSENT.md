# LocalSeek search study: information and consent

**What is this study?** We are students at Sardar Patel Institute of Technology, Mumbai. We built LocalSeek, an app that searches the files, apps and contacts on your phone without using the internet. We want to know how well its search works for people other than its developers.

**What will you do?** (about 45 minutes, plus leaving the phone to index, best overnight while charging)
1. Install the study version of LocalSeek and let it read (index) the files, apps and contacts on your phone. This happens only on your phone.
2. Type 10 to 15 searches for things you know are on your phone.
3. For each search, the app shows a list of results. Mark each one "Relevant" or "Not relevant".
4. Answer 10 short questions about how easy the app was to use.
5. Send us the result file the app creates.

**What we collect:** only numbers. These are the search quality scores per search, how long each search took, how many results you marked relevant, the type of each search (file, app or contact), how many words it had, how many files, apps and contacts were indexed, how long indexing took, battery level, your phone model and Android version, your answers to the 10 questions, and a random code such as P-7K3Q.

**What we never collect:** your name, phone number or email; the text of your searches; the names or contents of any file, app or contact; photos; your location. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything by itself. You will see the exact result file before you share it, and you choose how to send it.

**Your rights:** taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. You can ask us to delete your result file at any time by telling us your code. The "Delete study data" button removes everything the study stored on your phone, and you can uninstall the app afterwards.

**How the results are used:** combined results from all participants (averages and ranges, never one person's answers on their own) will appear in a research paper and in the project's public code repository.
