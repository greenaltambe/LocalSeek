# LocalSeek study: guide for participants

Thank you for helping. You need about **45 minutes of your time**, plus time for the phone to read your files (**best: leave it
charging overnight**). Nothing you type or any file name ever leaves your phone: the app has no internet permission. At the end
you send one small result file made of numbers only.

## 1. Install
1. You will get a file called `LocalSeek-study-<version>.apk` (WhatsApp, email or a cable).
2. Tap the file. If Android says it can't install apps from this source, tap **Settings** and switch on **Allow from this source** for
   the app you used (for example Files or WhatsApp), then go back and tap **Install**. You can switch it off again afterwards.
3. If Play Protect asks, choose **Install anyway** (the app is a university project, not from the Play Store).
4. Open **LocalSeek**.

## 2. Follow the screens
1. **Read and agree.** Tick "I agree" to continue. You get a random code like `P-7K3Q`; write it down.
2. **Allow access** to Files and Contacts when asked, then tap **Start indexing**. Indexing can take from a few minutes to some hours.
   Keep the phone **charging** and leave the app; your progress is saved. Come back later and the screen shows when it is finished.
3. **Practice search.** Type anything to see how judging works. It is not recorded.
4. **Your searches (10 to 15, at least 8).** For each one:
   - Think of something you **know is on your phone**: a file (a document, a PDF, notes), an app, or a contact.
   - Type the search the way you normally would. Mix styles over the study: sometimes just a name or keyword ("passport"), sometimes a
     sentence describing it ("the pdf with my bus pass rules"). Try files, apps and contacts.
   - Say what you were looking for (file / app / contact) and how you wrote it (keyword / sentence).
   - You see one list of results. For **every** item tap **Relevant** or **Not relevant**. Tap the item itself to open it and check.
     Be honest: if it is not what you wanted, it is "Not relevant", even if it looks close.
   - Say whether what you were looking for was in the list. Tap **Next**.
5. **Try it freely for 2 minutes.** Use the search like you normally would, then tap **Back to study**.
6. **Ten short questions** about how easy the app was to use.
7. **Send your result.** The screen shows the exact file. Tap **Share** and send it to the study organiser with any app you like.
   It contains only numbers, your code and your phone model.

## 3. Afterwards
- Tap **Delete study data** to remove everything the study saved on your phone. You can also clear the search index there.
- To remove the app: Settings > Apps > LocalSeek > Uninstall.
- You can stop at any time. To have your result deleted, tell the organiser your code.

## Tips
- Don't use searches for private things you would not want to open in front of the screen; the result file never contains them, but you
  will see them on screen.
- If a search finds nothing, the app tells you; just try other words.
- If the app is closed by Android while you judge, open it again: it continues where you stopped.
