LocalSeek Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

LocalSeek is a search app that works entirely on your device.

What LocalSeek accesses

With your permission, LocalSeek reads on-device data to build a search index:

  • Files on shared storage (names and paths of files, and extracted text of PDF and plain-text files),
  • Contacts (names and phone numbers/emails as stored in your address book),
  • Installed apps (names and package IDs),
  • Images (file names and on-device image embeddings).

Every permission is optional and is requested with an explanation. Search covers only what you allow.

Where it goes

Nowhere. The index is stored in the app's private storage on your device.

  • LocalSeek does not request the INTERNET permission and makes no network connections. You can verify this in Android's app info page for LocalSeek, under Permissions.
  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs are included.
  • Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the app, so the index is not copied to cloud backups.
  • The optional image search uses a model pack that Google Play downloads and installs on demand. LocalSeek itself never downloads anything.
  • Links inside the app (web search shortcuts, the project page) open in your browser; LocalSeek does not perform those requests.
  • We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Your control

  • Deny or revoke any permission in Android settings; search then covers only the data you allow.
  • Clear the index by clearing app storage or uninstalling. All data is deleted with the app.
  • Settings export (optional) writes engines, prefixes, pins and theme to a file you choose. It never includes your index, files or contact details, and contact pins are left out.

Children

LocalSeek does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes (for example, an opt-in online feature is added), this page and the store listing will be updated before release. Questions: open an issue at github.com/greenaltambe/LocalSeek/issues.
